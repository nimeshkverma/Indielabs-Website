About The Indie Labs

The Indie Labs started from a simple observation: advice is only as useful as the delivery that follows it. Too many recommendations are sound on paper but don't account for the people, systems and constraints that decide whether they work. We set up a firm that gives advice and also builds, so that both sides keep each other honest.

What we believe

Decisions over decks

The point of an engagement is a better decision, carried through. A document is a means to that end.

Evidence before opinion

We look at how things actually work before recommending how they should.

Say what we don't know

Where something is uncertain, we say so, and explain how to find out.

Leave clients more capable

Good work should reduce your need for us over time, not increase it.

A consultancy with a lab

The Indie Labs has two sides. The consultancy advises organisations on management, technology, product and AI. The lab builds our own SaaS and consumer products. Each side makes the other better: building products tests our advice against reality, and client work shows us problems worth solving.

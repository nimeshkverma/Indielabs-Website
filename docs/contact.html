Consulting enquiries

A few lines are enough to start. It helps if you include:

  • your organisation and your role
  • the situation or decision you're working on
  • any timeline or deadline
  • anything we should know before we talk, such as constraints or work already done

Product support

Our products are in development. If you're testing one and something isn't working, please include:

  • the product name
  • your device and operating system
  • what you expected, and what happened instead
  • the steps that led to the problem, plus screenshots if helpful

Please don't email health reports, prescriptions or other medical records. Describe the problem instead, and we'll ask for anything else we need.

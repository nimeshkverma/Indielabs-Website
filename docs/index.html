How an engagement runs

Every engagement follows the same shape, so you know what happens next and what you'll receive.

  1. Scope

    We agree the question to answer, the outputs and the timeline in writing.

  2. Discovery

    We talk to your people, review what exists and test our understanding with you.

  3. Delivery

    We produce the agreed outputs and work through them with your team.

  4. Handover

    Your team leaves able to carry the work forward without us.

Products

Alongside consulting, we build our own software. Building products keeps us close to the decisions our clients face: what to build first, what to leave out, and what it costs to run.

None of our products are publicly available yet. Each one below shows its current status.

  • Treasurywise

    Status: In development SaaS

    See all of your company's cash, deposits and investments in one place, and spot money that is sitting idle.

    Who it's for: Finance heads and founders at small and mid-sized companies whose money is spread across several banks and instruments.

    What we're building:

    • Balances across accounts and instruments, shown in a single view
    • Alerts when cash sits idle beyond a threshold you set
    • Reminders before deposits mature

    It gives you visibility. It doesn't recommend investments.

  • Ourdays

    Status: In development Consumer app

    One shared place for a couple to plan anniversaries, trips, family occasions and other events in their life together.

    Who it's for: Couples, particularly working couples, juggling two calendars and shared family commitments.

    What we're building:

    • A shared calendar for the occasions that matter to you both
    • Plans for trips and events that either of you can update
    • Reminders that reach both of you
    Register interest

  • Carefolio

    Status: In development Consumer app

    Bring your reports and prescriptions together into one organised health record that stays on your phone.

    Who it's for: People managing health paperwork for themselves or family members, such as someone coordinating care for elderly parents across several doctors.

    What we're building:

    • Add reports and prescriptions from photos or files
    • Records organised by date, type and family member
    • Everything stored on your device, not on our servers

    It organises your records. It doesn't interpret results or give medical advice.

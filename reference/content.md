## Page 2: Services

### 1. Intro

**Heading (H1):** Services

**Body:** We work with organisations of all types on the decisions that shape how they operate and what they build. Engagements are scoped around a specific question and a defined set of outputs, across four practices.

### 2. Management consulting

**Heading (H2):** Management
**Promise:** Clear priorities, decision rights and operating rhythm, so plans turn into execution.

**Where we help**
- Priorities are unclear, or every team has a different list.
- Decisions are slow, or keep escalating to the same few people.
- Plans are agreed but don't turn into consistent execution.

**Example engagements**
- **Operating model review.** We look at roles, decision rights, meeting cadence and reporting, and recommend specific changes.
- **Annual or half-year planning.** We help leadership set goals that teams can measure their progress against.
- **Leadership advisory.** We support leaders through a period of growth or restructuring as an ongoing sounding board.

**CTA:** Discuss a management engagement · `mailto:contact@indielabs.ai?subject=Enquiry%3A%20Management%20consulting`

### 3. Technology consulting

**Heading (H2):** Technology
**Promise:** Architecture and engineering decisions you can explain and defend.

**Where we help**
- Systems have grown without a plan and are becoming harder to change.
- A major platform or tooling decision is due, and the options are hard to compare.
- An investor, partner or acquirer needs an independent view of your technology.

**Example engagements**
- **Architecture review.** We assess your current systems and finish with a prioritised list of risks and fixes.
- **Build-versus-buy assessment.** We compare the options for a major platform or tooling decision on cost, risk, fit and long-term ownership.
- **Technical due diligence.** We give an independent assessment ahead of an investment, partnership or acquisition.

**CTA:** Discuss a technology engagement · `mailto:contact@indielabs.ai?subject=Enquiry%3A%20Technology%20consulting`

### 4. Product consulting

**Heading (H2):** Product
**Promise:** A product direction grounded in evidence, and a team set up to deliver it.

**Where we help**
- The team is building, but it's unclear which problems matter most to customers.
- The roadmap holds more than the team can deliver.
- There's no product lead, so specs, priorities and process are inconsistent.

**Example engagements**
- **Discovery sprint.** We run customer interviews, frame the problems and rank the opportunities.
- **Roadmap and prioritisation reset.** We help a team with too many competing demands agree what to do, in what order, and why.
- **Product process set-up.** We put specs, rituals and metrics in place for a team without a product lead.

**CTA:** Discuss a product engagement · `mailto:contact@indielabs.ai?subject=Enquiry%3A%20Product%20consulting`

### 5. AI consulting

**Heading (H2):** AI
**Promise:** AI applied where it clearly helps, with its risks and costs understood upfront.

**Where we help**
- There's pressure to "do something with AI", but no clear use case.
- A prototype works in a demo but isn't reliable enough to put in front of users.
- An AI feature is live, and there are concerns about its quality, safety or running cost.

**Example engagements**
- **AI opportunity assessment.** We rank candidate use cases by value, feasibility and risk.
- **Proof of concept with an evaluation plan.** We define what success looks like before building, then test against it.
- **AI feature review.** We assess an existing feature's output quality, guardrails and running cost, and recommend improvements.

**CTA:** Discuss an AI engagement · `mailto:contact@indielabs.ai?subject=Enquiry%3A%20AI%20consulting`

**Heading:** Ways to engage

## Page 5: Approach

### 1. Philosophy

**Heading (H1):** How we work

**Body:** Clients should know, at every stage, what we're doing, why we're doing it and what they'll receive. Three principles shape every engagement.

**Start with the decision**
We begin by agreeing the question the work needs to answer.

**Agree outputs upfront**
Deliverables, timeline and fee are set in writing before work starts.

**Work in the open**
You see our thinking as it develops, not just the final report.

### 2. Engagement lifecycle

**Heading:** From first email to handover

**1. Initial conversation**
We discuss the situation, what you've tried and what a good outcome would look like. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so.
*You receive:* a clear view of whether and how we can help.

**2. Proposal**
We write up the scope, approach, deliverables, timeline and fee.
*You receive:* a written proposal to review, adjust and approve.

**3. Discovery**
We talk to your people, review documents, systems and data, and test our understanding with you.
*You receive:* a short summary of what we found, confirmed with you before we go further.

**4. Delivery**
We produce the agreed outputs and work through them with your team.
*You receive:* the deliverables set out in the proposal.

**5. Handover**
We make sure your team can carry the work forward.
*You receive:* documentation, a handover session and an agreed point of contact for follow-up questions.

### 3. Ways of working

**Heading:** Ways of working

**Regular check-ins.** We meet three times a week to review progress, and send a written update after each check-in.

**One point of contact.** You'll always know who to speak to about the engagement.

**Across time zones.** We're based in India (IST, UTC+5:30) and arrange working hours to overlap with clients elsewhere.

### 4. Confidentiality and data handling

**Heading:** Confidentiality and your information

- We'll sign a mutual NDA before detailed discussions.
- We ask only for the access and information the work needs.
- We never use client information to build our own products.
- At the end of an engagement, we return or delete your information on request.

### 5. What we need from you

**Heading:** What we need from you

- **A sponsor** who owns the decision and can unblock the work.
- **Access** to the people, documents and systems in scope.
- **Timely feedback** at each checkpoint, so the work stays on track.
